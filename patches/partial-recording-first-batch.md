# First batch: partial-rep recordings

Record original camera video without app overlays, counters, skeletons, or banners. For each exercise below, use one side view that shows the working joints and equipment endpoints throughout the movement. Record two sets; each set has five full reps followed by five deliberate partial reps. Keep each cycle visible from start through return, and leave several seconds of still positioning before and after the set.

| Exercise | Camera | Sets | Per set |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Bench press | Side; show shoulder, elbow, wrist and bar endpoints | 2 | 5 full + 5 partial |
| Preacher curl | Side; show shoulder, elbow, wrist and handle | 2 | 5 full + 5 partial |
| Parallel-bar dips | Side; show shoulder, elbow, wrist and torso | 2 | 5 full + 5 partial |

Keep an independent written count and mark uncertain or incomplete cycles as ambiguous. Avoid relying on any tracker output for the labels. If a joint or endpoint is hidden, note when this happens. The total requested is six clips, 30 full reps and 30 partial reps.
