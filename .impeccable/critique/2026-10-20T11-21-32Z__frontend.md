## Cognitive load and emotional journey
Load is concentrated in calibration: five goals, five activity levels, seven weekdays and six condition choices, plus jargon. Natural multi-select groups and five labeled tabs are not automatic defects. Launch readiness warnings imply barriers before useful training; calibration demands commitment before a plan. Plans and quests create motivation, but timer-only training and unavailable analysis undermine the expected coach payoff. Scan review provides reassurance through explicit uncertainty and confirmation.
